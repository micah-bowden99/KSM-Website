Common Questions

How is this different from LinkedIn Learning or Coursera?

Generic platforms teach AI concepts. We teach your specific team how to use AI in their specific roles, with your specific constraints. Operations staff learn different things than analysts. And we're focused on one outcome: your team becoming self-sufficient.

We've tried AI training before and it didn't stick. What's different here?

Most training fails because it's theoretical. We include implementation guidance—helping your team actually use AI in their daily work, not just understand it in a classroom. We also match training to roles, so people learn what's relevant to them.

Our team is already stretched thin. How much time does this take?

The self-paced modules take 4-6 hours total per person, spread over weeks. The assessment call is 30 minutes. We designed this for busy teams—not as another thing competing for their time.

What happens after the free assessment?

We'll tell you what we found and make a recommendation. If we think training would help, we'll explain which tier makes sense. If we think you don't need us, we'll tell you that too. No obligation either way.

How quickly can we get started?

Small Business and Mid-Market tiers can start within a week of signing up. Enterprise LMS packages typically take 2-3 weeks to customise and deliver.

What if AI changes and the training becomes outdated?

Annual licensing includes updates. When the AI landscape shifts, we update the modules. You're not buying a snapshot—you're subscribing to current, maintained content.
