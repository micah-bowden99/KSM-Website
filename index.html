About Us

"A world where every Australian business confidently uses AI to its full potential."

Who We Are

We're BasePoint AI, founded by Micah and Tom – Bachelor-qualified engineers with Master's degrees in Project Management and direct manufacturing operations experience. We're not AI researchers selling theory, and we're not tech consultants selling dependency. We're practical implementers who've worked in real businesses and understand how operations actually function.

Our Purpose

We exist to educate, empower, and evolve Australian businesses toward AI independence. We believe businesses shouldn't need consultants forever – they need capability. Our job is to make your team competent and confident, not to create an ongoing revenue stream for ourselves.

Our Foundation

Because we're engineers, we understand how to implement complex solutions in real-world constraints. Because we come from operations and project delivery, we know the difference between impressive demos and sustainable processes. Because we value independence, we measure success by how quickly you don't need us anymore.
